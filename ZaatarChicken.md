# **Zaatar Chicken for 2**

## 🧂 **Ingredients**

* Chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on – 2 pieces (\~14 oz / 400 g)
* Za’atar – 1½ tbsp (9 g)
* Garlic – 2 cloves, minced (6 g)
* Lemon zest – 1½ tsp (3 g)
* Lemon juice – 1 tbsp (15 mL)
* Olive oil – 1½ tbsp (22 mL)
* Greek yogurt – 1 tbsp (15 g) *(optional)*
* Salt – ¾ tsp (4.5 g)
* Black pepper – ¼ tsp (0.5 g)

---

### 🔥 **Instructions**

1. **Marinate the Chicken**

   * Pat the chicken dry and lightly score the skin.
   * Mix za’atar, garlic, lemon zest/juice, olive oil, yogurt (if using), salt, and pepper.
   * Rub all over the chicken, including under the skin.
   * Marinate covered in the fridge for **at least 2 hours**, ideally overnight.

2. **Cook (Roast or Grill)**

   * Let marinated chicken sit at room temperature for 30 minutes before cooking.
   * **Oven**: Preheat to **425°F (220°C)**. Place chicken skin-side up on a rack or cast iron skillet. Roast **35–40 minutes** or until 165°F (74°C) internal temp.
   * Optional: Broil for 2 minutes for crispy skin.
   * **Grill**: Cook over medium indirect heat until nearly done, then crisp over direct heat.

3. **Rest & Serve**

   * Rest for 5–10 minutes.
   * Garnish with parsley, a touch of olive oil, and more za’atar if desired.
