# 🧅 Sumac Onions Recipe (Quick-Pickled Style)

### Ingredients:

* Red onion – 1 medium, thinly sliced (\~150 g)
* Ground sumac – 1 tbsp (6 g)
* Lemon juice – 2 tbsp (30 mL)
* Olive oil – 1 tbsp (15 mL)
* Salt – ¾ tsp (4.5 g)
* Optional: chopped parsley or mint (1 tbsp / 2 g), a pinch of chili flakes

---

## 🔪 Method:

1. Thinly slice the red onion into half-moons.
2. In a bowl, toss onions with salt and let sit 5 minutes to begin softening (draws out moisture).
3. Add lemon juice, sumac, and olive oil. Toss well.
4. Let sit at least 15–30 minutes. The onions will soften, mellow, and turn a **garnet hue** from the sumac.
5. Add herbs before serving for freshness.

---

## 🔍 Why It Works:

* **Salt + acid denature onion enzymes**, reducing harshness while preserving crunch.
* **Sumac reinforces citrus notes** and introduces subtle fruitiness.
* Balances za’atar chicken by adding **brightness and textural contrast**.

---

## 🔄 Variations:

* Add **thinly sliced cucumbers or radishes** for extra crunch.
* Use **white vinegar (1 tbsp / 15 mL)** if you’re short on lemon juice.
* Want it quicker? Massage the onions vigorously with the sumac-lemon mix for 1 minute to accelerate softening.

These onions keep well for 2–3 days refrigerated. Would you like a version with fermented or longer-pickled onions for more complexity?
