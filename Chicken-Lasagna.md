# Creamy Chicken & Mushroom Lasagna Recipe

Ingredients

250g jumbo precut mushrooms

250g Italian veggie mix (e.g., zucchini, bell peppers, onions, carrots)

300g minced chicken

1 bottle passata (around 700g)

50ml white wine

2 cloves garlic, minced

½ tsp chili flakes

Fresh pasta sheets

Shredded mozzarella (less than 150g, to preference)


For the Béchamel

250ml milk

100ml cream

20g butter

1 tbsp flour

¼ tsp nutmeg

1 clove garlic, minced

Salt & pepper to taste


Instructions

1. Sauté the Vegetables

Heat a pan with some olive oil.

Add mushrooms, cook until browned, then set aside.

In the same pan, cook the Italian veggie mix until soft. Set aside.



2. Cook the Chicken Sauce

In the same pan, add minced chicken and cook until lightly browned.

Add minced garlic, chili flakes, salt, and pepper. Stir for 1 minute.

Pour in white wine and let it reduce.

Add passata and simmer for 10–15 minutes.



3. Make the Béchamel

Melt butter in a saucepan. Add flour, stir until golden.

Add minced garlic and cook briefly.

Gradually whisk in milk and cream, stirring constantly.

Add nutmeg, salt, and pepper. Cook until thick.



4. Assemble the Lasagna

Spread a thin layer of chicken sauce in a baking dish.

Add pasta sheets, a layer of béchamel, then veggies and mushrooms.

Repeat layers, finishing with béchamel on top.

Sprinkle shredded mozzarella to preference.



5. Bake

Cover with aluminum foil and bake at 180°C (350°F) for 25 minutes.

Remove foil and bake for another 10–15 minutes until golden and bubbling.



6. Rest & Serve

Let it sit for 10 minutes before slicing. Enjoy!
